# Weekly Client Update Email

## Rules

1. **Plain text only** - no markdown formatting (bold, italic, links). Gmail renders it as raw text.
2. **Use "-" for dashes** - not "--" or em dashes.
3. **Project-based sections** - organize by project, not by day or task type.
4. **Name people** - reference specific team members involved (builds trust, shows engagement).
5. **Always include hours** - current hours vs 40-hour cap, through what date. Flag early if trending over.
6. **Overage options** - when near/over cap, present options: pause until next month, raise cap, or shift items.
7. **No cross-client references** - never mention other clients or their projects.
8. **Personal touch** - reference recent conversations or shared context.
9. **Keep it scannable** - short bullets, not paragraphs. Busy people skim.
10. **Send as draft** - never send directly. Create Gmail draft for review.
11. **Subject line** - "Weekly update" or "[Project] Weekly Update - [Date]".
12. **Timing** - every Monday per recurring tasks.

## Template

```
Hi [Name],

Hope you had a good weekend. Quick update on where things stand:

[Project 1]
- [Status/progress on main deliverable]
- [Key milestone hit or blocker resolved]
- [Issue flagged, if any]

[Project 2]
- [Status/progress update]
- [Decision needed or dependency, if any]

On hours - timesheet shows [X] of the 40-hour cap for [Month] through [MM/DD]. [If near/over cap, present options:]
1. Prioritize remaining hours on highest-impact items and pause until [next month]
2. Temporarily raise the cap given current momentum
3. Keep the cap but shift lower-priority items to [next month]

This week
- [Next priority or deliverable]
- [Action items from last week's discussions]

Let me know if priorities have shifted or if I'm missing anything.

Best,

--
[Sender Name]
```
